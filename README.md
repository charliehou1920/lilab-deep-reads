# Li Lab Deep Reads

Weekly deep reads of new papers for the Zhe Li Lab (Brigham and Women's Hospital / Harvard
Medical School), on BRCA1 and mammary epithelium, ESR1 signaling in fallopian tube and ovarian
cancer stroma, IGF1R signaling, and extrachromosomal DNA.

The site is static: `index.html` plus one JSON file per week under `data/`. It is generated by
the lab's private digest pipeline and published here with GitHub Pages. Nothing here is edited
by hand.

Each entry is our own summary of a published paper: the research question, the methods in plain
language, the key figures explained, the findings with their evidence, the limitations, and a
logic chain and knowledge graph of the argument. Every paper links to the original, which is
where the authoritative version lives. Figure images are reproduced only for open-access papers
and preprints; for subscription papers the figures are explained in our words and the images are
left to the publisher.

Summaries may contain errors. Read the original before citing anything.
